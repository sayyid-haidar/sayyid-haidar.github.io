# Portfolio & Knowledge Studio

A personal publishing context for presenting selected engineering work and durable technical knowledge.

## Language

**Knowledge Entry**:
A published cheatsheet or long-form writing entry that preserves practical engineering knowledge.
_Avoid_: Content, post, note

**Project**:
A selected engineering case study presented separately from Knowledge Entries.
_Avoid_: Knowledge Entry, post

**Project Cover**:
A 16:9 visual preview for a Project, authored at an intrinsic size of 1600×900 and cropped when necessary.
_Avoid_: Hero image, thumbnail
